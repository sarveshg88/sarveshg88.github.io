Method

Ten symptoms, one cause.

A chemistry lab taught me the order: observe, isolate the variable, react, then calibrate. It turns out product work runs the same way.

01

Observe

Gather every symptom before naming a solution.

50+ interviews at Tayog, 28K+ reviews on one marketplace.

02

Isolate

Separate the one cause from the noise around it.

5 failure modes at Amex, traced to one.

03

React

Build one thing that removes the cause, not five patches for its symptoms.

A 6 module platform that replaced 4 tools.

04

Calibrate

Set thresholds by what each error costs, then tune against a baseline.

A 5:1 cost matrix, and a 3 tier policy tuned by grid search.

Problem
Find the customers who won’t buy again across 3.4M orders and 206K users.
Insight
